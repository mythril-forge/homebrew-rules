# Stat Generation
## Rules as Written
In 5e, generating stats is plain and simple, as explained from the rules below.

> ## Determine Ability Scores
> &hellip;
>
> You generate your character's six ability scores randomly.
> Roll four 6-sided dice and record the total of the highest three dice on a piece of scratch paper.
> Do this five more times, so that you have six numbers.
>
> ### Standard Array
> If you want to save time or don't like the idea of randomly determining ability scores, you can use the following scores instead: 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8.
> Now take your six numbers and write each number beside one of your character's six abilities to assign scores to Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma.
> Afterward, make any changes to your ability scores as a result of your race choice.
>
> &mdash; Player's Handbook, page 12


## Mythril Forge Ruleset
With the Mythril Forge ruleset, this method has changed slightly.
Rather than rolling 4d6 and dropping the lowest die, you will be rolling 6d4 and dropping the highest die.
Additionally, the standard array has changed from [15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8] to [15, 13, 12, 11, 9, 7].

Rolling this set of dice will allow you to get a higher maximum (20 vs 18) while restricting a higher minimum (5 vs 3).
At the same time, it does consciously lower the average of your rolls by one point.
This change is made to compensate for improvements made elsewhere in the ruleset.

> ## Determine Ability Scores
> You generate your character's six ability scores randomly.
> Roll six 4-sided dice and record the total of the lowest five dice on a piece of scratch paper.
> Do this five more times, so that you have six numbers.
>
> ### Standard Array
> If you want to save time or don't like the idea of randomly determining ability scores, you can use the following scores instead: 15, 13, 12, 11, 9, 7.
> Now take your six numbers and write each number beside one of your character's six abilities to assign scores to Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma.
> Afterward, make any changes to your ability scores as a result of your race choice.

Note that the point-buy variant rule is not permitted.
