# Stat Generation
## Rules as Written
In 5e, generating stats is plain and simple, as explained from the rules below.

> [vanilla]

## Mythril Forge Ruleset
With the Mythril Forge ruleset, this method has changed slightly.
Rather than rolling 4d6 and dropping the lowest die, you will be rolling 6d4 and dropping the highest die.
Additionally, the standard array has changed from [15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8] to [15, 13, 12, 11, 9, 7].

Rolling this set of dice will allow you to get a higher maximum (20 vs 18) while restricting a higher minimum (5 vs 3).
At the same time, it does consciously lower the average of your rolls by one point.
This change is made to compensate for improvements made elsewhere in the ruleset.

> [homebrew]

Note that the point-buy variant rule is not permitted.
