## Determine Ability Scores
You generate your character's six ability scores randomly.
Roll six 4-sided dice and record the total of the lowest five dice on a piece of scratch paper.
Do this five more times, so that you have six numbers.

### Standard Array
If you want to save time or don't like the idea of randomly determining ability scores, you can use the following scores instead: 15, 13, 12, 11, 9, 7.
Now take your six numbers and write each number beside one of your character's six abilities to assign scores to Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma.
Afterward, make any changes to your ability scores as a result of your race choice.
